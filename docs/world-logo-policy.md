# WORLD list brand marks

The14 WORLD cards show each source site's actual brand mark, not Open Graph artwork or cropped social cards. `world-logos.json` records each source, method and output hash.

- Unmodified official SVGs: EXPRESSION,くらんぽ,酸冷交代浴,Book Keeping,OMOCHI TOUCH.
- SUBURBIA CLASSICA: original owner-supplied logo JPEG, not the derived OGP; original colours retained.
- Faithful outline exports from current DOM/font sources: CRATEDIGGIN', Premium Cuts Listed., earth number, my Chems, 一見坊の【日本妖怪学体系】, 花鳥風月、そして魚と虫, 掌蹠膿疱症.com. They are exports of existing wordmarks, not newly proposed identities.
- 1minutes.: current Arial wordmark reproduced as HTML on its existing dark-colour swatch.

All frames use the portal's quiet neutral background. Optical width/height limits vary with the original mark's shape; original colours and letter proportions are not changed. The source brands remain independent. Earth Number uses its current live wordmark while the separate proposed wordmark selection is pending; no unselected proposal is presented as final.

The parent portal's MINA identity, Open Graph/Twitter cards,14 destination URLs, descriptions, category filters and navigation are unchanged. Older imported OGP files remain archived with their original provenance but are not referenced by WORLD cards. Font licenses used for outline exports are in `world-logo-licenses/`; no fonts are fetched at runtime and no CSP permissions are broadened.
